What it does

Everything a chicken keeper actually needs

🥚

Weekly Egg Log

Tap-to-log weekly grid. See at a glance who laid what, who's slowing down, and how the whole flock is doing.

🐔

Hen Profiles

Give every hen her own page — name, breed, hatch date, egg color, leg band, her story, personality, and a photo.

📋

Daily Coop Journal

Simple checklist for daily chores — water, feed, treats, nest boxes, predator check, and how the flock is looking.

💊

Medication Records

Log treatments per bird or whole flock. Egg withdrawal periods show up in the egg log so you never collect a withdrawal egg by mistake.

🏆

Wall of Fame

Hen of the Week, Hen of the Month, Best Hen of All Time — automatic awards for your top layers.

🔒

Your Data is Yours

Everything stays on your phone. No accounts, no cloud, no ads. PRO users can back up and restore anytime.

Hen Profiles

Know every hen, track every egg

Every hen has a story. Give her a profile and that information travels with every egg she lays — so you always know who's who, even years later.

🏷️

Name & Breed

From Rhode Island Reds to Silkies — pick from a curated list or add your own. Optional names so it's fine if she hasn't earned one yet.

📖

Her Story

Where she came from, distinguishing features, personality, favorite treats — the small things that make her her.

❤️

Active · Retired · At Rest

Hens move through life. Retire her from the egg log when she's done laying, or mark her At Rest when her time comes — her record stays.

📸

Photos (PRO)

Take a photo with the camera or pick from your gallery. A small portrait at the top of her profile and on the hen list.

How it works

Three steps to a well-kept flock

1

Set up your coop

Name your coop, add your hens, and you're ready to log. No accounts, no setup wizard — open the app and go.

2

Log eggs & chores daily

A tap or two per hen for eggs. Quick checklist for the day's coop chores. Takes a minute, not ten.

3

Watch patterns over time

Browse weekly history, see your top layers, spot trends. Catch problems early. Watch your flock thrive.
